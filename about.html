Our Story

From A Single Mat To A Sanctuary Of Thousands

stackly began as a sunset class for neighbours under the same banyan tree where our founder first learned pranayama from her grandmother. Week by week the circle grew - until the forest asked to be carved into a studio.

We built stackly on three promises: honour the tradition, respect every body, and keep the doors open to anyone seeking stillness. Twelve years later those promises are still our compass - now with bamboo floors, rainforest showers and a community that feels like family.

Rooted in Giving

Every quarter we offer free community classes and fund rural yoga teacher training.
